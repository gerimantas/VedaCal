D6 · Graph variants

Five ways to draw the same day — Vilnius, Saturday 10 October 2026, same data and verdicts as D5. One cursor moves through all of them: tap or drag on any chart.

A · Colour strip with soft edges

From weather hour strips: Mercury Weather, Sparrow, Blue

One slim line of colour; neighbouring parts melt into each other instead of hard steps. The good windows are named under it with their times. Smallest of all — fits above the day list. Hides why; the readout tells it.

B · Smooth curve, coloured by height

From the iOS Weather UV chart, Weather Line and the RISE energy curve

Hills are good times, valleys are times to avoid; the colour follows the height, from green at the top to red at the bottom. The past is dimmed. The steps of D5 become slopes, so the eye reads a shape, not a staircase. The level is still one of four — the curve only rounds the corners.

C · 24-hour ring

From sun clocks and wheels: Sloww, yatanco/solar, Vedic Yantra (Choghadiya wheel), Lunoris (solunar dial)

Noon at the top, midnight at the bottom; the thin gold arc outside is daylight. The wide ring is the verdict with soft edges; the three thin rings inside are the inputs (day base, Choghadiya, fixed windows) with hard edges. The hand points at the cursor, the centre says what it means. Close to the app's existing dial. 24 hours only — the last 1 h 46 min of D5 (next morning) is left out.

D · The sun's path, coloured

From sunrise widgets and sun-path dials

The line is the sun: it rises above the horizon at sunrise and sinks under it at sunset. Its colour is the verdict. Day and night need no legend. The height is the sun, not the verdict — so only colour carries the answer.

E · Heat rows — every activity and every input

From heatmaps (horizon charts, GitHub contributions) and Oura's day timeline

One thin row per activity group, then one row per input — all on the same hours. Compare activities at a glance: where they differ is the day base. Only two groups are worked out so far; the other eight would be eight more rows. The activity switch above does not change this chart.

Times are Drik Panchang's. Verdicts follow SPEC 13.4 and reproduce worked example 1 (.planning/research/examples.md). Gulika is disputed: drawn faint, never decides. Concept sketches for Q3, not the app. "Now" for dimming in B is fixed at 13:15.
