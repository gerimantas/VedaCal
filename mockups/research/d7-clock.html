D7 · The day as a 24-hour clock

Vilnius · one turn of the hand = one day · 12 at the top, 00 at the bottom · tap or drag anywhere on the dial

Hand

  • Thin ring 1 — fixed windows. Red: Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Dur Muhurtam, Varjyam, junctions, noon and midnight. Green: Abhijit, Amrit Kalam, Brahma Muhurta. Decides.
  • Thin ring 2 — day base. Weekday, tithi, nakshatra, yoga: grey neutral, orange poor, red avoid. Decides.
  • Thin ring 3, faded — Choghadiya. Popular practice without a classical source. Shown only, does not decide.

Settings

In the app this lives on the Settings screen (user, 2026-10-10).

Colours

Current is the default.

Times are Drik Panchang's. Verdicts follow SPEC 13.4; 10 October is worked example 1 (.planning/research/examples.md), 10 May is worked the same way from its Drik fixtures. Gulika is disputed: drawn faint, never decides. Choghadiya is practice (R21): its ring is shown half-faded and never decides (user, 2026-10-10). The dial shows 24 hours from the start of Brahma Muhurta; the rest of the night up to the next sunrise is in the readout only. Concept sketch for Q3, not the app; built on D6 variant C. "Now" is fixed at 13:15.
